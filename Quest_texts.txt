Oath Descriptions
Here are thematic descriptions for each oath, suitable for a character creation screen.

Oath of Mercy

“For the Children of Ilúvatar, both the Firstborn and the Followers, hold a fate beyond the circles of this world. To spill their blood in anger is to bring a shadow upon your soul. This oath you shall swear: to turn your blade from Elf and Man, and in so doing, find a grace that violence cannot afford.”

Oath of Silence

“It is said that much was wrought in the Elder Days by thought and not by voice. In the deep places of the earth, and in the silent watch of the stars, true power resides not in song but in solemn purpose. Swear this oath: to forgo all songs of power, and let your deeds alone be your voice. In that stillness, you shall find an unlooked-for strength.”

Oath of Iron

“Let the darkness gather and the fires of the deep rage. Let despair be your companion and all hope seem lost. There is a will that does not bend, a spirit that will not break. Swear this oath: to never seek escape, to never ascend toward the light, until you have reclaimed a Silmaril from the shadow. In this doom, your heart shall be hardened as the foundations of the mountains.”

Oath of the Smith

“The craft of the hand is the gift of the Maker. To wield a blade forged in another's fire is to trust a stranger's heart. True mastery is born of one's own sweat and skill. Swear this oath: to bear no weapon and wear no mail save that which you have wrought yourself. By this covenant, the secrets of anvil and fire shall be laid bare to you.”

3. Aule the Smith
Quest Dialog:

The air is warm with the heat of a distant forge, and a voice like the ringing of a hammer on an anvil speaks. Aule the Smith, the Maker, regards you with a deep and knowing gaze.

"'I have seen your works, mortal. I have seen the stone you have hewn and the metal you have shaped. There is a spark of the fire of creation within you.'"

"'But a spark is not a flame. Skill must be honed by trial. The substance of the earth does not yield its secrets gladly. Show me that you are a master of your craft.'"

"'Here is my forge. Its fires burn with the heat of the world's heart. Take up the hammer, and from the base matter of this place, create for me a thing of worth and purpose. Let me see the skill of your hands and the ingenuity of your mind.'"

Completion Dialog (New):

You lay your finished work before the Smith. Aule takes it in his mighty hands, his gaze missing no detail. He turns it over, feeling its weight, its balance, its very spirit.

"'It is well-wrought. The hand that made this knows the song of the metal. You have not merely forced its shape, but listened to the substance and persuaded it to your will. This is true craft.'"

"'You are worthy of my blessing. May your arm never tire at the anvil, and may the hidden flaws of rock and steel ever reveal themselves to your eye.'"

4. Nienna, Lady of Pity
Quest Dialog:

A soft, sorrowful light gathers in the gloom. In its midst stands a figure cloaked in grey, and her eyes hold the memory of every grief the world has ever known. It is Nienna, the Weeper, and her voice is as gentle as falling rain.

"'Child, this place is filled with pain. The creatures that dwell here are not all born of evil, but are twisted and marred by the darkness they have long endured. They are lost, and in their fear, they lash out.'"

"'I have wept for them, as I weep for all the sorrows of Arda. If there is yet pity in your heart, I ask of you a difficult task.'"

"'Find the path downward, but raise no hand against the tormented things that dwell here. Show them a mercy they have never known. Let compassion be your shield and your guide. As a sign of my faith in you, the ways of descent shall be revealed to your sight.'"

Completion Dialog:

As your foot touches the first step of the downward stair, a feeling of peace washes over you, and the gentle voice of Nienna returns.

"'You have chosen pity over violence. You have walked through the valley of fear and left behind not death, but a glimmer of hope. Such a deed is a light in the deepest darkness.'"

"'For the mercy you have shown to the lost, I grant you this blessing: may your path be shrouded in silence, and your footsteps fall without echo when compassion is your guide.'"

A tear, shining like a star, falls upon the stone and vanishes. The presence of Nienna fades, leaving you with her quiet blessing.
