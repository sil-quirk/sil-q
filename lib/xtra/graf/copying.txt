Sil-More Graphics Notice
========================

Current public releases of Sil-More do not include Adam Bolt's 16x16 tiles
or David Gervais's 32x32 tiles.

The current main gameplay tileset (`16x16.png`) is the MicroChasm tileset
used for Sil-More.

Additional wall and environment artwork used by the project comes from the
following asset packs purchased on itch.io for Sil-More:

 * Retro Dungeon Tileset and Sprites by Wolffius
   https://wolffius.itch.io/dungeon-tileset-and-sprites

 * Dungeon Tileset Mega Pack by Pine Druid
   https://pine-druid.itch.io/dungeon-tileset-mega-pack

 * The Roguelike by Backterria
   https://backterria.itch.io/the-roguelike

 * Starter Tiles by Ninjikin
   https://ninjikin.itch.io/starter-tiles

These purchased packs are used as incorporated game assets, primarily for
wall and environment graphics.  Do not redistribute the raw source packs
separately from the game.  Keep the purchase records for these packs with the
project's release/compliance records.

If future releases add or restore third-party tiles beyond the set above,
update this file before distribution.
