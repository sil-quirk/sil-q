Sil-More Sound and Music Notice
===============================

This folder contains a mixture of bundled gameplay sound effects, project
music, and reference/source-pack material.

Gameplay sound effects in the shipped game are drawn from the following
sources:

 * RPG Audio by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl), licensed under CC0.
   See `License.txt` in this folder.

 * Free Fantasy 200 SFX Pack by TomMusic.
   The included `ReadMe.rtf` states that the pack is royalty-free for
   commercial and non-commercial projects, with credit appreciated, but not
   for resale or redistribution as a standalone asset pack.

 * Inventory SFX Pack by LeoHPaz.
   Purchased for Sil-More from itch.io:
   https://leohpaz.itch.io/inventory-sfx-pack

 * Mini Fantasy Weapons SFX Pack by LeoHPaz.
   Purchased for Sil-More from itch.io:
   https://leohpaz.itch.io/minifantasy-weapons-sfx-pack

 * Individual swoosh effects by qubodup / Iwan Gabovitch.
   See `swoosh.txt` in this folder.

Music:

 * The main theme and death theme were composed specifically for Sil-More by
   sinefabula and are used by permission for the game and soundtrack
   publication by arrangement with the composer.
   Public artist page:
   https://open.spotify.com/artist/6SehByz91EUfWCwOzYP9Ys

 * The ambient track "Grey Hour" was written by West Wind and gifted for use
   in Sil-More.  Keep the direct permission record with the project's
   release/compliance records.
   Public track page:
   https://soundcloud.com/west-winnd/grey-hour

The `packs/` directory contains source/reference material and is excluded from
the clean release build created by `create-release-build.ps1`.

Do not redistribute any third-party source pack or standalone soundtrack asset
outside the game unless you have the relevant separate rights to do so.
