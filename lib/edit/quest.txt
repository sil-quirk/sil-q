# File: quest.txt

# This file is used to initialize the "q_info" array, which is used
# to store quest information for Sil-Q.

# === Understanding Quest Information ===

# Q: Quest index : Quest name
# T: Title text
# C: Challenge description
# O: Associated oath (OATH_SILENCE, OATH_SMITH, OATH_IRON, OATH_MERCY, or NONE)
# Y: Quest type (0=vault-based, 1=roulet-based)
# S: Stat bonuses (str:dex:con:gra)
# K: Skill bonuses (skill:bonus pairs)
# A: Special abilities granted
# I: Initialization/quest giving text lines
# W: Completion/reward text lines

# Version stamp (required)
V:0.8.7

# Quest of Tulkas the Strong
Q:1:Tulkas the Strong
T:The Champion of Valinor seeks a worthy opponent
C:Defeat the randomly assigned enemy that challenges your might
O:OATH_SILENCE
Y:1
S:1:0:0:0
A:9:5
I:Tulkas the Strong, mightiest of the Valar in deeds of prowess,
I:stands before you with a grin of anticipation.
I:
I:"Ho there, brave one! I have long sought a mortal worthy
I:of true challenge. Face the enemy I shall name, and prove
I:your valor in battle!"
I:
I:"Should you succeed, I shall grant you a treasure forged
I:in the fires of Valinor itself, and unlock the Oath of
I:Silence for your future endeavors."
W:Tulkas roars with delight as you return victorious:
W:
W:"Magnificently done! Your prowess rivals the greatest
W:champions of old. Accept this gift, wrought in the
W:smithies of the Blessed Realm!"
W:
W:"You have also proven worthy of the Oath of Silence -
W:the path of the grim and wordless warrior is now open
W:to you in future lives."
