### New Common Monsters

Based on the analysis of the existing `monster.txt` file, I've focused on creating suggestions that fit the First Age lore from Tolkien's works (primarily *The Silmarillion*, *The Lays of Beleriand*, and early drafts in *The History of Middle-earth*). New monsters are either directly mentioned (e.g., specific creatures from battles or regions) or inferred closely from the lore (e.g., corrupted variants of existing beings, like twisted elves or additional elemental spirits under Morgoth's influence). I've avoided anything anachronistic (e.g., no Second/Third Age elements like Nazgul).

I prioritized balance and variety:
- **Depth and Rarity**: Assigned based on existing patterns (e.g., weaker at shallow depths, stronger deeper; rarity 1-3 for commons).
- **Flags and Attributes**: Mirrored similar existing monsters (e.g., orcs are SMART, HURT_LITE; undead are RES_COLD, NO_FEAR).
- **Themes**: Expanded on underrepresented groups like corrupted elves/men, insects, elemental horrors, and variants of serpents/dragons to fill gaps without overlap.
- **Letters/Symbols**: Used consistent with file (e.g., 'o' for orcs, 'H' for horrors, '@' for humanoids).
- **Uniques vs. Commons**: Commons are non-unique, repeatable spawns.

### Lore Analysis: Utumno Era

Based on a deep inspection of Tolkien's lore (primarily *The Silmarillion*, *The History of Middle-earth* series, especially *The Book of Lost Tales* and *Morgoth's Ring*, and cross-referenced via reliable sources like Tolkien Gateway and scholarly discussions), Utumno represents Melkor's primordial phase of corruption, emphasizing cold, ice, darkness, and discord over the later fiery themes of Angband. Key points:

- **Utumno's Nature**: Located in the far north under the Iron Mountains (Ered Engrin), it was a vast, cold fortress with deep pits, vaults, and caverns. *The Silmarillion* (Ch. 1-3) describes it as a place of "cold and dim light" where Melkor's discord created extremes of heat and cold, but cold dominated (e.g., blizzards, frosts opposing the Valar's lamps). After its destruction in the War of Powers, remnants hid in the earth, influencing later evils.

- **Creatures and Beings**:
  - **Demons/Fallen Ainur (Umaiar)**: Primary inhabitants were corrupted Maiar/Ainur spirits who allied with Melkor early (*The Silmarillion*, Ch. 3; *Morgoth's Ring*, Myths Transformed). These "demons" became like him in corruption, often shadowy or elemental. In *The Book of Lost Tales* (HoMe Vol. 1-2), early drafts mention "demons of cold" or ice-related horrors as part of Melkor's host, tied to his discord creating "unwholesome colds" and phantoms. No named cold demons, but they contrast later fire Balrogs.
  - **Monsters of Corruption/Mockeries**: Melkor bred nameless beasts in mockery of Eru's creations (*The Silmarillion*, Ch. 2). These were primordial, not yet orcs/trolls/dragons (bred later). HoMe suggests twisted spirits, phantoms, and "ogres/goblins" (early orc-like). Cold themes: Ice-bound horrors, frozen mockeries (e.g., corrupted beasts adapted to northern desolation).
  - **Orcs' Origins**: First Elves captured and corrupted here (*The Silmarillion*, Ch. 3), implying early twisted humanoids in cold pits.
  - **Hidden Survivors**: Post-destruction, many creatures fled into earth's depths (*The Silmarillion*, Ch. 3), becoming "nameless things" or lurking evils.
  - **Themes**: Cold/ice (freezing pits, northern blight), darkness (shadowy spirits), primordial horror (pre-fire era, elemental discord). No dragons/vampires yet; focus on spirits, phantoms, corrupted elementals.

Gaps/Expansions: Lore is sparse on specifics, allowing close inferences like cold Raukar variants (extending file's Raukar as demonic Maiar). Avoid anachronisms (e.g., no Second Age elements). Suggestions below emphasize Utumno's cold/dark primacy, fitting as deeper dungeon monsters (depth 15+ for "ancient" feel).
